Water and Waste Management Requirements

Businesses using substantial quantities of water or generating wastewater, solid waste, hazardous waste, or other waste may be subject to additional requirements.

Requirements may depend on:

- Water consumption
- Wastewater generation
- Type of waste
- Hazardous materials
- Production process
- Industry
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Water connection permission.

Groundwater extraction permission where applicable.

Wastewater discharge permission.

Solid waste authorization.

Hazardous waste authorization.

Waste management registration.

Potential approval types:
- Water Use Permission
- Groundwater Permission
- Wastewater Discharge Approval
- Solid Waste Authorization
- Hazardous Waste Authorization
- Waste Management Registration

The business should verify applicable requirements with the relevant water, environmental, municipal, or local authority.
