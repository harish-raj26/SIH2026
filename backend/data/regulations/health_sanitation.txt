Health and Sanitation Requirements

Businesses involving food, healthcare, public interaction, manufacturing, accommodation, or other activities affecting public health may be subject to health and sanitation requirements.

Requirements may depend on:

- Nature of business
- Number of employees
- Public access
- Food handling
- Healthcare activities
- Sanitation facilities
- Waste generation
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Health trade licence.

Sanitation approval.

Health inspection.

Medical establishment registration where applicable.

Public health clearance where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Health Trade Licence
- Sanitation Approval
- Health Inspection Certificate
- Medical Establishment Registration
- Public Health Clearance

The business should verify applicable requirements with the competent health or local authority.
