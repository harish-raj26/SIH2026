Environmental and Pollution Control Requirements

Businesses that may generate air emissions, wastewater, hazardous waste, solid waste, noise, or other environmental impacts may require environmental permissions.

Requirements may depend on:

- Industry type
- Pollution category
- Production capacity
- Air emissions
- Water consumption
- Wastewater generation
- Hazardous waste
- Fuel usage
- Chemicals used
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Consent to Establish.

Consent to Operate.

Environmental clearance where applicable.

Hazardous waste authorization where applicable.

Waste management authorization where applicable.

Air emission related permissions where applicable.

Water discharge related permissions where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Consent to Establish
- Consent to Operate
- Environmental Clearance
- Hazardous Waste Authorization
- Waste Management Authorization
- Air Pollution Permission
- Water Pollution Permission

The applicable requirement should be verified with the relevant environmental authority.
