Factory Licence Regulatory Guidance

A factory licence is required for applicable manufacturing establishments operating under the relevant factories and labour regulations.

Manufacturing establishments may be required to obtain approval from the competent factories and labour authority before commencing regulated operations.

The applicant may need to provide information relating to the business entity, factory premises, manufacturing activities, number of workers, machinery and other prescribed information.

The competent authority may inspect the premises before granting approval where inspection is applicable.

The licence may be subject to renewal or periodic compliance requirements according to the applicable rules.

Businesses should verify the current requirements, forms, fees and submission procedure from the official authority before applying.
