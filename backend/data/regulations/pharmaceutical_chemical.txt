Pharmaceutical and Chemical Industry Requirements

Businesses manufacturing, processing, storing, importing, or distributing pharmaceutical products, chemicals, hazardous substances, or controlled materials may require additional regulatory permissions.

Requirements may depend on:

- Type of product
- Chemical classification
- Hazard classification
- Quantity stored
- Manufacturing process
- Storage facility
- Production capacity
- Worker exposure
- Environmental impact
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Drug manufacturing licence.

Chemical handling authorization.

Hazardous substance storage permission.

Chemical manufacturing approval.

Pharmaceutical product registration.

Controlled substance authorization where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Drug Manufacturing Licence
- Chemical Handling Authorization
- Hazardous Substance Storage Permission
- Chemical Manufacturing Approval
- Pharmaceutical Product Registration
- Controlled Substance Authorization

The business should verify applicable requirements with the competent pharmaceutical, chemical, environmental, or safety authority.
