Labour and Employment Requirements

Businesses employing workers may be subject to labour and employment requirements.

Requirements may depend on:

- Number of employees
- Type of employment
- Nature of work
- Working hours
- Wages
- Workplace conditions
- Industry
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Employee registration.

Establishment registration.

Labour welfare registration.

Employment-related licences or registrations.

Workplace safety compliance.

Contract labour requirements where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Labour Registration
- Establishment Registration
- Labour Welfare Registration
- Contract Labour Registration
- Workplace Safety Approval

The business should verify applicable requirements with the competent labour authority.
