Product Licensing and Certification Requirements

Businesses manufacturing, importing, distributing, or selling regulated products may require product-specific licences, registrations, certifications, or approvals.

Requirements may depend on:

- Product category
- Intended use
- Ingredients or materials
- Manufacturing process
- Product risk
- Production capacity
- Import or export activity
- Location
- Applicable sector regulations

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Product manufacturing licence.

Product registration.

Product certification.

Product conformity approval.

Import-related product authorization.

Sector-specific product approval.

Potential approval types:
- Product Manufacturing Licence
- Product Registration
- Product Certification
- Product Conformity Approval
- Product Import Authorization
- Sector-Specific Product Approval

The applicable requirement should be verified with the competent sector regulator or certification authority.
