Land Use and Building Requirements

Businesses establishing premises may require permissions relating to land use, construction, building plans, occupancy, or changes to an existing property.

Requirements may depend on:

- Location
- Land-use classification
- Building area
- Construction type
- Building height
- Intended activity
- Zoning
- Local authority rules

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Land-use approval.

Building plan approval.

Construction permission.

Occupancy certificate.

Completion certificate.

Trade premises permission.

Potential approval types:
- Land Use Approval
- Building Plan Approval
- Construction Permission
- Occupancy Certificate
- Completion Certificate
- Trade Premises Approval

The business should verify requirements with the relevant local planning or municipal authority.
