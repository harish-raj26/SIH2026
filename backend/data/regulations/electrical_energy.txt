Electrical and Energy Requirements

Businesses using significant electrical loads, electrical installations, generators, boilers, or other energy systems may require applicable permissions or inspections.

Requirements may depend on:

- Connected electrical load
- Type of electrical installation
- Generator capacity
- Machinery
- Industrial activity
- Building type
- Energy source
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Electrical installation approval.

Electrical safety inspection.

Electrical connection permission.

Generator permission.

Boiler-related approval where applicable.

Energy-related registration where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Electrical Installation Approval
- Electrical Safety Certificate
- Generator Permission
- Boiler Approval
- Energy Registration

The business should verify applicable requirements with the competent electrical, energy, or local authority.
