Transportation and Storage Requirements

Businesses transporting, storing, warehousing, or distributing goods may be subject to transportation, warehouse, storage, or safety requirements.

Requirements may depend on:

- Type of goods
- Quantity
- Hazard classification
- Storage capacity
- Vehicle type
- Transportation activity
- Warehouse type
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Warehouse registration.

Storage licence.

Commercial vehicle permit.

Goods transportation authorization.

Hazardous material transportation permission.

Cold storage registration where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Warehouse Registration
- Storage Licence
- Commercial Vehicle Permit
- Goods Transportation Authorization
- Hazardous Material Transport Permission
- Cold Storage Registration

The business should verify applicable requirements with the competent transport, storage, local, or sector authority.
