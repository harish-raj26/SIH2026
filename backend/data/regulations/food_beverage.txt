Food and Beverage Requirements

Businesses involved in manufacturing, processing, storing, distributing, serving, or selling food and beverages may be subject to food safety requirements.

Requirements may depend on:

- Type of food
- Type of beverage
- Manufacturing activity
- Production capacity
- Food handling activity
- Storage activity
- Distribution activity
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Food business registration.

Food manufacturing licence.

Food processing approval.

Food storage permission.

Food distribution registration.

Product-specific food authorization where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Food Business Registration
- Food Manufacturing Licence
- Food Processing Approval
- Food Storage Permission
- Food Distribution Registration
- Product-Specific Food Authorization

The applicable requirement should be verified with the competent food safety authority.
