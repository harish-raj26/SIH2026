Packaging and Labelling Requirements

Businesses placing packaged products on the market may be subject to packaging, labelling, marking, declaration, and product information requirements.

Requirements may depend on:

- Product category
- Packaging type
- Quantity
- Intended consumer
- Product ingredients
- Country of origin
- Manufacturing information
- Sector-specific rules

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Packaging registration.

Product labelling compliance.

Packaged commodity registration.

Mandatory product declarations.

Sector-specific labelling approval where applicable.

Potential approval types:
- Packaging Registration
- Product Labelling Compliance
- Packaged Commodity Registration
- Product Declaration Approval
- Sector-Specific Labelling Approval

The business should verify applicable requirements with the competent regulatory authority.
