Fire and Building Safety Requirements

Businesses operating from commercial, industrial, storage, or public premises may be subject to fire safety requirements.

Requirements may depend on:

- Building size
- Building height
- Occupancy
- Number of employees
- Nature of activity
- Storage of combustible materials
- Storage of hazardous materials
- Fire load
- Location

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Fire NOC.

Fire safety approval.

Fire safety certificate.

Fire protection system approval.

Inspection by the competent fire authority.

Potential approval types:
- Fire Safety Approval
- Fire NOC
- Fire Safety Certificate
- Fire Protection System Approval

The business should verify applicable requirements with the competent fire and rescue authority.
