Factory and Industrial Establishment Requirements

Industrial establishments may require permissions or registrations before establishing or operating manufacturing activities.

Requirements can depend on:

- Nature of manufacturing activity
- Number of workers
- Use of machinery
- Power consumption
- Factory premises
- Production capacity
- Hazardous materials
- Location
- Applicable state requirements

Potential regulatory requirements may include:

Factory Licence.

Industrial establishment registration.

Factory plan approval.

Permission for installation or operation of specified machinery.

Permission relating to hazardous manufacturing activities where applicable.

Manufacturing establishments should determine the applicable requirements with the competent labour, factory, industrial, or local authority.

Potential approval types:
- Factory Licence
- Factory Plan Approval
- Industrial Establishment Registration
- Machinery Permission
- Hazardous Process Permission
